Before you start: router and DNS checklist
  1. Point a domain or subdomain (for example planner.yourname.com) at your home's public IP address. If your IP changes, use a dynamic DNS service such as DuckDNS or your registrar's DDNS.
  2. On your router, forward outside port 80 → this server's port 8080, and outside port 443 → port 8443. Don't forward to TrueNAS's own 80/443; those belong to the TrueNAS web UI.
  3. Save the domain below and click Check port 80. Some routers can't test their own public address from inside your network, so a failed check doesn't always mean it won't work.
  4. Click Test with staging first. It uses Let's Encrypt's test service, which has generous retry limits. Once that works, click Get real certificate.
